Revenue → Cost Sankey Builder

Set your revenue sources and cost items, generate the chart, then screenshot it for your slides.

Structure

Costs are subtracted in the order listed, left to right, each one peeling off what's left after the previous one.

Title

Shown as the chart's title, with the revenue/cost/net tally underneath as a subtitle. One comparison period applies to the whole chart: fill the "Prior" column for every source and cost, and each node shows its % change under its name. Leave a Prior cell blank and the nodes that depend on it show no %.
